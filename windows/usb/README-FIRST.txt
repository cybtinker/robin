Fledgling Live - USB stick
==========================

Fledgling Live puts your voice makeup into Zoom, Teams, Discord and Meet on a Windows 10/11 PC.

EASIEST: double-click "Setup Fledgling Live.exe".
  - It installs Fledgling Live for you (no administrator rights needed for the app).
  - If the PC has no virtual microphone yet, it also offers to install VB-CABLE.
    VB-CABLE is a driver, so Windows asks for ADMINISTRATOR RIGHTS (a UAC prompt) and,
    when it is done, for a RESTART. On a work PC you may need your IT team for this step.

WITHOUT INSTALLING (Portable folder):
  1. Right-click "Install VB-CABLE.cmd" and choose "Run as administrator" (only once per PC),
     then restart the PC.
  2. Double-click FledglingLive.exe. Settings are kept on this PC, not on the stick.

In your call app, choose "CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)" as the microphone.

About VB-CABLE: VB-CABLE is donationware by VB-Audio Software, not part of Fledgling.
It is included here unchanged, as VB-Audio allows. If you use it, please support its author
at www.vb-cable.com. To remove it later, run Portable\VB-CABLE\VBCABLE_Setup_x64.exe as
administrator and choose "Remove Driver".
